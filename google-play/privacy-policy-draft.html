Rental Report Privacy Policy

Developer: 久燕 王 (Jiuyan Wang). Updated: October 12, 2026.

Data processed on your device

The app processes property details, notes, photographs and typed participant sign-offs to provide its features. The app does not use advertising or analytics, and does not require a user account. We do not sell your information or automatically upload this content to us.

Sharing you choose

When you choose to share or export, Android and your selected destination app handle the transfer. Their policies apply to that transfer. You control what information to include.

Permissions

Camera and system photo-picking features are used only when you choose to add an image. Photos and signatures may contain personal information; include only what you need.

Storage and removal

Inspection records and copies of attached photos are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Exported PDFs are generated locally; files you save or share outside the app are controlled by you and the destination app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local records. Copies already exported or shared must be removed separately. Android system backup and device transfer may copy app data according to your device settings and system behavior; we do not operate that backup service.

Children

The app is intended for adult users and is not designed for children.
