Rental Report helps landlords, tenants and property hosts document a rental walkthrough in a clear room-by-room report.

START THE RIGHT INSPECTION
Choose move-in, move-out or routine inspection and add the property name and address.

RECORD EACH ROOM
Review the room checklist, add notes and attach photos from the camera or photo picker. Mark items as good, needing cleaning, worn, damaged or missing.

TRACK THE DETAILS
Add issue notes and severity while you walk through the property. Inspection records and attached photos are saved locally so you can return to your work after restarting the app.

COMPLETE THE HANDOFF
Record participant names and confirmation dates as typed sign-offs. Review the inspection summary before exporting.

EXPORT A PDF
Generate a report with property details, room conditions, notes, room photos and typed sign-offs. Share the exported file using the system share sheet. Choose whether to include untouched rooms in the report.

Core inspection features work offline without an account. The app includes no ads or subscriptions.

Rental Report is a documentation tool. It does not provide legal advice, guarantee a deposit outcome or replace a locally required inspection form. Use the records as part of your own documentation process.
